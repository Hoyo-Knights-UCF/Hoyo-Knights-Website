Group Gaming Sessions | Group Discussions | Character Builds | Play Throughs | Watch Alongs | Social Events | and More!


Join us for an Adventure Packed, Genshin Impact Multiplayer CO-OP Session! Bring Your Laptops, Tablets, Playstations w/ Monitors? And get ready to have some fun!


We will get into groups of 4 and join worlds. Higher ranked players can join lower ranked worlds. Players can join worlds with a World Level equal to or lower than their own. Higher-level players are welcome to help newer Travelers!


If needed, you may download Genshin Impact here.


Notes

Seating is limited and will be on a first-come, first-served basis.

Bring a blanket and a small pillow if you would like, just in case you are not able to secure a seat!



  • Adventure Rank 16 or higher is required to participate in Co-Op Mode.


  • Complete the Archon Quest Prologue:

    • Act I – The Outlander Who Caught the Wind to unlock Co-Op Mode.



  • Make sure you have access to the Co-Op menu before attending!


  • All participants must be playing on the North America (NA) server to join one another.


  • Please have your UID (User ID) ready so other members can add you.


  • Some quests and activities temporarily disable Co-Op Mode.

    • Make sure you aren't in the middle of a quest that prevents multiplayer access.



  • Bring your favorite characters and be ready to have fun!
